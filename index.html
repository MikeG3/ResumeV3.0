About Me

Engineering has never been just a profession to me, it has been the way I naturally interact with the world.

If something breaks, I want to know why. If something works exceptionally well, I'm curious how it was designed. And if I think it can be improved... I usually can't resist trying.

"Curiosity has always been my most powerful tool."

As a kid, taking things apart was rarely optional—it was almost inevitable. Fortunately, I also became reasonably good at putting them back together again. Over time that curiosity evolved from building and repairing electronics into designing entirely new systems.

After working with industrial automation, I realized I wanted to understand not only how machines worked, but why they worked the way they did. That decision eventually led me back to university, where I earned a Bachelor of Engineering in Software Engineering while continuing to work professionally.

Combining hands-on technical experience with formal engineering education gave me a perspective that bridges theory and practical implementation. Throughout my career I have worked in startups, manufacturing, financial services, consulting, enterprise environments and independent engineering. Every industry solved different problems, but each reinforced the same lesson:

"Good engineering begins by understanding people and their needs"

Outside of work, I'm rarely far from another project. Sometimes thats building live-edge furniture, fixing an old car, experimenting with photography, or seeing if another bonsai tree can survive one of my "engineering improvements."

I also enjoy hiking, camping, cycling, fitness, music & guitar, kayaking remote islands and coastlines, and generally finding excuses to build, repair or learn something new.

I don't believe engineering is simply about writing code or designing hardware in isolation.

It's about working with people, solving meaningful problems, and creating solutions that genuinely improve someone's life.
